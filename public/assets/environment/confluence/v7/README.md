# Cyberundead drainage kit v7

Built-in imagegen output based on the approved water concept sheet in `docs/Worldbuilding/water-concepts/water-studies-v1.png`. The original is preserved in `sources/drainage-source-v7.png`; `drainage-v7.png` and `drainage-v7.json` are the runtime atlas and tight frame manifest.

Four generated modules: electronic sluice, straight downpipe, downward outlet elbow and masonry catch-basin rim. A fifth atlas frame crops the straight pipe's middle into a repeatable shaft without open end caps. Packing preserves the generated alpha; `scripts/pack-confluence-drainage.mjs` also produces a white-background inspection image.

Infrastructure art contains no baked water. Runtime water, falling motion, impacts, currents and reflections are separate from the sprite art and from gameplay physics. The kit is optional; missing art does not prevent play.

## Exact generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Production sprite atlas for the cyberundead side-view platformer in the reference concept sheet. Reference is style only. Generate one square transparent PNG with EXACTLY FOUR isolated architectural sprites in a regular 2 by 2 grid, each entirely inside its own equal square quadrant with generous transparent margins, no labels, no backdrop, no floor, no shadows outside objects. Crisp detailed pixel art, front orthographic side-view game perspective, blue-black industrial steel, dark blue masonry, restrained cyan electronic indicators and thin magenta electrical accents. No brass, gears, steam, or steampunk. Top left: wide rectangular wall-mounted electronic sluice gate/outfall frame, dark open mouth at its bottom center, thick masonry jambs, circuit panel at side, source for a broad waterfall, but NO water painted in. Top right: tall straight narrow segmented drainage downpipe with mounting collars, clearly visible top and bottom pipe ends, no elbow, neutral blue steel with cyan glints. Bottom left: compact ninety-degree pipe elbow / outlet spout, vertical inlet at top, bend toward viewer and downward-facing dark open mouth at lower end, flange and bracket; NO water. Bottom right: wide shallow masonry catch-basin front and side rim, a trough with open transparent interior for separately rendered water, drainage notch at right, straight-on game view, attached dark metal braces. Match reference material detail and silhouette but supply standalone reusable game assets only. All four objects isolated with true alpha transparency. Water, splashes, mist, and scenery will be animated separately in code, do not include them.
