# Confluence · canal-side menu study

A visual concept for the next main menu: Cole and the skeletal cat beside a cyan canal, beneath Confluence's electronic blue-glass station canopy. Quiet dark space on the left supports the title and traditional menu. The city continues behind them through railways, occupied rooms, conduits and spectral waterways.

[menu-concept-v1.png](menu-concept-v1.png) is a flattened design study, generated with the **built-in imagegen tool**. The [exact prompt](menu-concept-v1.prompt.txt) and [provenance](provenance-v1.json) preserve its inputs and original output path. The selected PNG is copied unchanged into this folder.

References: the actual `environment/confluence/v3/city-v3.png` background and `v6/canopy-v6.png` module, the user's canonical Cole sheet, and `hero/canon-v1/idle-v1.png`. The study explores composition and atmosphere; it is not a tile-for-tile runtime render. It does not replace the active menu.

For production, assemble the station from the current Confluence materials and dedicated matching foreground pieces. Keep Cole as authored complete-character frames. Separate water currents/reflections, outfall impacts, flame/scarf loops, cat motion and the occasional distant train from stationary architecture. A menu-owned decorative timeline stays independent of gameplay simulation and saves.
