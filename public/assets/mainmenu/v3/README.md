## Production layer breakdown

These are planned drawing layers, not files hidden inside the flattened PNGs:

1. Distant city, rails and atmosphere, with clear separation by depth.
2. Station architecture, platform paving and fixed prop supports, including clean backing where moving parts will expose it.
3. Contact/cast shadows and local light masks for the hero, lantern and nearby materials.
4. Detailed hero body with planted boots, registered skull/flame attachment and neck/cape seam.
5. Authored flame drawings and separate cloth drawings, preserving detail and silhouette between frames.
6. Lantern housing and emission mask with a real link pivot; stationary post, hook and mounting foot.
7. Foreground leaves and selected prop edges for occlusion. Later station work adds clock hands, cat, souls and atmosphere.
8. Existing DOM title, controls and dialogs, kept separate from all scene images. The composition study's departure board is blank; its lettering will be composed separately.

## Review and next step

Compare the studies with the original at normal browser size and at native resolution. Look at material detail, the hero's lean proportions, local light direction, contact with the paving and whether the city retains its scale. The corner is deliberately a closer composition, so it establishes detail treatment rather than the final on-screen figure size.

The full composition also explores the later clock, bench and cat arrangement; those props have no new production animation assets yet. Future layer construction must retain this level of drawing and consistent proportions. A detailed flattened image is a visual reference, rather than proof that its parts are ready to animate.
